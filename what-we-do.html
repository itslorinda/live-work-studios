What We Do

Good spaces support great lives.

Kickstand Design Company designs buildings, writes about how to live in them well, and makes the objects that finish a room.

Kickstand started from a simple observation: most buildings ask you to choose. Live here, work somewhere else. Gather in a space that doesn't feel like your own. We design buildings that mix commercial and residential space with intention, not compromise — ground floor for the work, the floor above for the life that comes after it.

That same thinking carries into everything else we make. The articles we publish are the real notes from real projects — materials we've used, products we've specified, mistakes we'd fix next time. The art and lighting we sell come out of the same studio that draws the floor plans, made with the same eye for what a room actually needs.
